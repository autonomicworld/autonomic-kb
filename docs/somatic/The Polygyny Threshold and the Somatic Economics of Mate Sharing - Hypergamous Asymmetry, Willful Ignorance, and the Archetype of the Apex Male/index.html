2.1 Humans as Mildly Polygynous Primates

Biologically and taxonomically, Homo sapiens are neither strictly monogamous (like gibbons) nor promiscuously polyandrous (like bonobos). Humans are natively mildly polygynous, serial pair-bonding primates4:

  1. Sexual Dimorphism: Human males are on average 15-20% larger in body mass, possess 45-50% greater upper-body muscle mass, and exhibit significantly higher bone density and spatial navigation capabilities than females5. In primatology, physical dimorphism is the definitive anatomical signature of intrasexual male competition for multi-female access.
  2. Testicular and Sperm Mechanics: Relative testicular mass to body weight in human males sits precisely between solitary monogamous primates (gorillas) and multi-male promiscuous primates (chimpanzees)6, signaling an evolutionary adaptation for pair-bonding punctuated by polygynous competition.
  3. The 80/40 Genetic Reproduction Asymmetry: Comprehensive genomic sequencing across global populations (Karmin et al., Genome Research 2015)7 proves that across human history, roughly 80% of women successfully reproduced, whereas only 40% of men passed on their genes. During the agricultural revolution, this bottleneck tightened to a staggering 1 male reproducing for every 17 females, as dominant warlords hoarded land, resources, and female harems.

1. Greco-Roman "Civic Monogamy" (Athens & Rome)

Greco-Roman law mandated that a citizen man could have only one legal wife (matrimonium) to produce citizen heirs8. However, Greco-Roman monogamy was hyprocritical and incomplete: men were legally permitted and culturally expected to use slave concubines, prostitutes (hetairai), and junior males. Absolute fidelity was legally demanded of the wife, but the husband enjoyed recognized, institutionalized sexual access to low-status females9.

2. Imperial Chinese "Normative Monogamy with Legal Concubinage" (Yi Qi Duo Qie)

From the Han to the Qing Dynasties, Chinese law strictly enforced One Primary Wife (Qi). A man who attempted to elevate a secondary woman to Primary Wife was severely punished. However, wealthy and elite males were legally permitted and socially encouraged to take multiple Concubines (Qie)10. Concubines occupied a lower legal rank, but their children were legitimate heirs to the family estate. Imperial China thus operated an explicit, legal harem system layered under a single primary wife title.

3. Islamic and Ancient Near Eastern Frameworks

Islamic jurisprudence placed legal caps on male harem size—limiting men to a maximum of 4 legal wives provided they received equal financial support, alongside slave concubinage (Ma malakat aymanukum)11. Biblical Near Eastern traditions similarly recognized polygyny and concubinage (e.g., Kings David and Solomon). In Judaism, polygyny was only formally banned in Europe around 1000 AD via the Ban of Rabbi Gershom (Herem de-Rabbeinu Gershom)—a decree heavily influenced by the surrounding European Christian legal environment12.

4. The Western Christian Innovation: Universal Absolute Monogamy

What Joseph Henrich (The WEIRDest People in the World)13, Walter Scheidel (Sex and Empire)14, and Laura Betzig (Despotism and Differential Reproduction)15 identify as uniquely radical about the Western Church's Marriage and Family Plan (MFP) (initiated around 400 AD and consolidated in the Gregorian Reforms of ~1000 AD) was that it created Universal Absolute Monogamy without Legal Concubinage, enforced equally across peasants, lords, and kings:

  • Banned Kin-Group Cousin Marriage: Extended consanguinity bans up to the 7th degree dissolved powerful polygynous clan networks, preventing wealthy dynasties from hoarding women and capital within kin circles.
  • Confronted Monarchs: The Church repeatedly risked major wars and excommunicated European monarchs (e.g., King Lothair II, King Philip II of France, and King Henry VIII) when kings attempted to repudiate wives or take recognized concubines.

By forcing even the most powerful Apex Males in Europe to forfeit legal multi-female harems, the Western Church executed the most complete Level 3 Social pacification project in human history:
