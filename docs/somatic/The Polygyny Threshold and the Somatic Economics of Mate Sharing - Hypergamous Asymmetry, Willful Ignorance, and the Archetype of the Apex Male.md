### 2.1 Humans as Mildly Polygynous Primates

Biologically and taxonomically, _Homo sapiens_ are neither strictly monogamous (like gibbons) nor promiscuously polyandrous (like bonobos). Humans are **natively mildly polygynous, serial pair-bonding primates**[4](#user-content-fn-4):

1. **Sexual Dimorphism:** Human males are on average 15-20% larger in body mass, possess 45-50% greater upper-body muscle mass, and exhibit significantly higher bone density and spatial navigation capabilities than females[5](#user-content-fn-5). In primatology, physical dimorphism is the definitive anatomical signature of intrasexual male competition for multi-female access.
2. **Testicular and Sperm Mechanics:** Relative testicular mass to body weight in human males sits precisely between solitary monogamous primates (gorillas) and multi-male promiscuous primates (chimpanzees)[6](#user-content-fn-6), signaling an evolutionary adaptation for pair-bonding punctuated by polygynous competition.
3. **The 80/40 Genetic Reproduction Asymmetry:** Comprehensive genomic sequencing across global populations (Karmin et al., _Genome Research_ 2015)[7](#user-content-fn-7) proves that across human history, **roughly 80% of women successfully reproduced, whereas only 40% of men passed on their genes**. During the agricultural revolution, this bottleneck tightened to a staggering **1 male reproducing for every 17 females**, as dominant warlords hoarded land, resources, and female harems.

#### 1. Greco-Roman "Civic Monogamy" (Athens & Rome)

Greco-Roman law mandated that a citizen man could have only one legal wife (_matrimonium_) to produce citizen heirs[8](#user-content-fn-8). However, Greco-Roman monogamy was **hyprocritical and incomplete**: men were legally permitted and culturally expected to use slave concubines, prostitutes (_hetairai_), and junior males. Absolute fidelity was legally demanded of the wife, but the husband enjoyed recognized, institutionalized sexual access to low-status females[9](#user-content-fn-9).

#### 2. Imperial Chinese "Normative Monogamy with Legal Concubinage" (_Yi Qi Duo Qie_)

From the Han to the Qing Dynasties, Chinese law strictly enforced **One Primary Wife** (_Qi_). A man who attempted to elevate a secondary woman to Primary Wife was severely punished. However, wealthy and elite males were legally permitted and socially encouraged to take multiple **Concubines** (_Qie_)[10](#user-content-fn-10). Concubines occupied a lower legal rank, but their children were legitimate heirs to the family estate. Imperial China thus operated an explicit, legal harem system layered under a single primary wife title.

#### 3. Islamic and Ancient Near Eastern Frameworks

Islamic jurisprudence placed legal caps on male harem size—limiting men to a maximum of 4 legal wives provided they received equal financial support, alongside slave concubinage (_Ma malakat aymanukum_)[11](#user-content-fn-11). Biblical Near Eastern traditions similarly recognized polygyny and concubinage (e.g., Kings David and Solomon). In Judaism, polygyny was only formally banned in Europe around 1000 AD via the **Ban of Rabbi Gershom (Herem de-Rabbeinu Gershom)**—a decree heavily influenced by the surrounding European Christian legal environment[12](#user-content-fn-12).

#### 4. The Western Christian Innovation: Universal Absolute Monogamy

What Joseph Henrich (_The WEIRDest People in the World_)[13](#user-content-fn-13), Walter Scheidel (_Sex and Empire_)[14](#user-content-fn-14), and Laura Betzig (_Despotism and Differential Reproduction_)[15](#user-content-fn-15) identify as uniquely radical about the Western Church's **Marriage and Family Plan (MFP)** (initiated around 400 AD and consolidated in the Gregorian Reforms of ~1000 AD) was that it created **Universal Absolute Monogamy without Legal Concubinage**, enforced equally across peasants, lords, and kings:

- **Banned Concubinage and Bastard Inheritance:** Unlike Rome, China, or Islam, the Church outlawed all secondary wives, slave concubinage, and royal mistresses. Children born outside legal monogamous marriage (_bastards_) were legally barred from inheriting titles, land, or estates.
- **Banned Kin-Group Cousin Marriage:** Extended consanguinity bans up to the 7th degree dissolved powerful polygynous clan networks, preventing wealthy dynasties from hoarding women and capital within kin circles.
- **Confronted Monarchs:** The Church repeatedly risked major wars and excommunicated European monarchs (e.g., King Lothair II, King Philip II of France, and King Henry VIII) when kings attempted to repudiate wives or take recognized concubines.

By forcing even the most powerful Apex Males in Europe to forfeit legal multi-female harems, the Western Church executed the most complete **Level 3 Social pacification project** in human history:

- **Suppression of Male Variance:** Redistributed female sexual access to median and lower-tier males.
- **Halting Young Male Violence:** Eliminating the massive surplus of un-mated, sexually excluded young males drastically reduced inter-tribal raiding, blood feuds, and internal violence (_Young Male Syndrome_, Daly & Wilson)[16](#user-content-fn-16).
- **Enabling High-Trust Civilization:** Lower-tier males, assured of paternity certainty and pair-bonded mates, invested their surplus labor into long-term economic building rather than violent conquest, laying the high-trust foundation for modern Western economic growth.

Why does the female nervous system execute willful ignorance rather than immediate confrontation?

1. **The High Cost of Explicit Knowledge:** In legal and social frameworks, _explicit acknowledged knowledge_ forces action. Once an affair is openly admitted or proved beyond plausible deniability, social scripts mandate confrontation, ultimatum, or divorce.
2. **Confrontation as a Trajectory Threat:** Issuing an ultimatum to an Apex Male (_"It's her or me!"_) carries extreme risk. Because an Apex Male possesses high optionality, he is highly likely to reject the ultimatum and dissolve the pair-bond.
3. **Somatic Preservation of Level 3 Rank:** A divorce strips the primary female of her elevated social rank, coalitional leverage, luxury home ecosystem, and exclusive access to the family wealth. Her autonomic nervous system parses this sudden loss of trajectory as an existential threat to her and her children's safety.
4. **Strategic Subcortical Blindness:** Willful ignorance is an evolved somatic defense mechanism. By remaining in a state of un-investigated suspicion, the female preserves **plausible deniability**. She maintains her Level 3 Social title as "The Wife," preserves her children's inheritance priority, and avoids the catastrophic allostatic shock of dyadic collapse.
